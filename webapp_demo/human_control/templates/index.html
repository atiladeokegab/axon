Once it is up

  1. The session opens its own console window. Click it — that is where the keyboard goes.
  2. Its 3D twin opens in a browser tab, showing what the system believes the arm is doing.
  3. Press A to arm. Nothing stimulates until you do.
  4. ↑↓ raise and lower · ←→ swing forward and back · WS bend and straighten the elbow · G grip · X emergency stop.
